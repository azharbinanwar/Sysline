The part other monitors get wrong

It counts what actually
left your Mac.

Traffic between apps on your own machine — a simulator streaming its screen, a local database, a dev server — never touches your connection. Most tools count it anyway, and your day reads 270 GB.

Sysline measures only the real interfaces: Wi‑Fi, ethernet, hotspot, VPN. Your data plan and your history agree.

Where it all lives

On your Mac. That's the whole list.

One file, yours

Everything sits in a single database in your Application Support folder. No server, no account, no sign-in.

Nothing phones home

Sysline talks to the network for two things only: a speed test you started, and checking whether a new version exists.

It stays small

Detailed samples are kept for two days and then folded into hourly totals, so a year of history is still a few megabytes.
